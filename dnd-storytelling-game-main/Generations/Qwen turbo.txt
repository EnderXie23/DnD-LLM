World Setting:
Nova Terra.
Nova Terra is a post-apocalyptic world transformed into a vast network of high-tech cities interspersed with desolate wastelands. Once a vibrant planet, it now stands as a testament to human hubris and the indomitable spirit of survival. The cities, known as Arcologies, are towering structures designed to house millions and are powered by advanced fusion reactors and renewable energy sources. Between these urban sanctuaries lie barren expanses, scarred by the fallout of nuclear wars, where the remnants of nature struggle to reclaim what was lost.
In the wake of global devastation, society has adapted to a highly advanced technological paradigm. The economies of the arcologies thrive on the development and distribution of artificial intelligence, nanotechnology, and robotics. AI systems manage everything from resource allocation to city governance, ensuring efficiency and sustainability. However, remnants of pre-war technologies and artifacts from the wastelands are highly valued, leading to a thriving black market economy. Nuclear power remains a contentious resource, harnessed carefully to prevent further environmental degradation.
Nova Terra's society is stratified, with a stark divide between those living in the arcologies—privileged citizens with access to the latest technology—and the scavengers and survivors eking out a living in the wastelands. Within the arcologies, governance is handled by AI councils, but human oversight ensures moral accountability. The wasteland dwellers, often viewed as outcasts, form tight-knit communities and possess a profound resilience and ingenuity, utilizing salvaged tech and scavenged materials.
The inhabitants of Nova Terra include a mix of humans, genetically enhanced beings, and sentient robots. Humans are predominantly found within the arcologies, while the wastelands are home to scavengers and mutants. Sentient robots and androids, equipped with advanced AI, serve various roles from maintenance workers to combat units. Mutations resulting from radiation exposure have led to diverse human variations, some possessing extraordinary abilities.
Advanced technologies and AI augment the capabilities of individuals. Within the arcologies, residents can enhance their physical and mental capacities through neural implants and cybernetic enhancements. In the wastelands, survivors rely on resourcefulness and ingenuity, employing makeshift tech solutions and salvaged equipment. Mutants may exhibit unique biological adaptations, granting them heightened senses or physical prowess. AI entities can manipulate technology remotely, providing strategic advantages in various scenarios.
Nova Terra was once Earth, until the outbreak of a catastrophic nuclear war that ravaged the planet. In the aftermath, humanity began rebuilding, driven by the need for survival and a desire to prevent such devastation again. The arcologies emerged as bastions of technology and safety, governed by AI to ensure stability. Over time, these cities grew in sophistication, while the wastelands became havens for outcasts and pioneers, each striving to carve out their own destiny amidst the ruins of a shattered world.
