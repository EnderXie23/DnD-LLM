Module Introduction
The oldest dragons are a brotherhood, and for good reason. In the world of Elara, dragons once dominated the land, sky, and sea. However, as millennia passed, their numbers dwindled, and in some places, they survive only in legend. The dragons watched as elves rose to become the world's new masters, took notice of the short-lived humans, and observed the emergence of green-skinned races like goblins, orcs, and trolls, who presented new challenges to the world order.
Yet dragons are a resilient race. Their eldest wield great magic and hide in concealed lairs, pondering the future, interpreting omens, and crafting plans to avert their extinction. Through carefully chosen agents, they attempt to pit younger races against one another, ally with elves in magical unions, and ensure the survival of their kind - perhaps even reclaiming dominance.
Among the elder dragons, a troubling prophecy has emerged: a human hero will rise, destined to unite humanity against the dragon-elf alliance, even rallying many green-skinned races under their banner. Though this hero's arrival might still be centuries away, the elder dragons vigilantly search for clues about the hero's bloodline to prevent the prophecy from unfolding.

DM Background
In the adventure book The Goblin King's Slave Mines, a valley 250 miles south of the Bronsman Kingdom is described. Dragons have often laid their eggs there, but goblins recently occupied the valley. They discovered that consuming dragon blood grants them dragon-like traits, leading to the rise of a hybrid species called dragon-goblins, blending goblin chaos with draconic resilience.
Before this adventure, dragon-goblin shamans uncovered traces of the human hero prophesied in the dragons' lore. Unlike dragons, these shamans lack patience and foresight, panicking and performing dangerous divination rituals to pinpoint the hero's origins. Many shamans perished from their reckless magic use, but survivors believed they identified the hero's bloodline in a family living in the southern village of Craecia, near the goblin-controlled border.
Armed with this information, the goblin shamans devised a plan to thwart the prophecy. They chose three dragon-goblin champions and infused them with more dragon blood, creating monstrous entities. These three were sent north to Craecia to slaughter the prophesied human hero's family, leaving only the youngest daughter alive. She would be captured and taken to the dragon-goblin valley for magical testing to confirm the prophecy and then sacrificed to the goblin demon god.

Starting Point
Players arrive in Craecia the morning after the attack on the mayor's house, finding the family murdered and the youngest daughter, Liza, kidnapped. Villagers mourn their loss, unprepared to pursue the dragon-goblins into the south. If players agree to rescue Liza, the villagers provide basic supplies and offer a pooled reward of 983 gold pieces for her safe return.

Monsters
The primary foes are the three dragon-goblin champions detailed at the adventure's end, along with standard dragon-goblins described in The Goblin King's Slave Mines. DMs should familiarize themselves with these creatures to maximize the challenge.

Day 1 - Crossing South of the Village
South of the village, the land becomes increasingly barren, the terrain more rugged, the forests denser, and the paths more treacherous. You struggle through this area, scanning the sky through occasional clearings between the trees for signs of the "dragon people" and their captive. By noon, doubts creep in as to whether this rescue is a futile endeavor.
Then, at a clearing by a stream, you discover a fragment of rose-colored silk under a rock. The fabric is too fresh to have been there long - it must have been torn from the captive girl's clothing and deliberately placed as a sign. Next to the ribbon, you spot a faint human handprint in the soil, one finger pointing southward. It seems this girl, Liza, is smarter than expected! She must have left this clue while her captors paused to rest or drink. Additionally, it appears that the "dragon people" are not fast flyers, as your pursuit seems to be closing in on them.
If the player characters search the area for additional clues, allow each of them to roll a DC 15 Investigation check. A success reveals further evidence - broken branches, disturbed stones, or partial footprints - indicating that the "dragon people" have started traveling on foot, which should make tracking them easier.

Days 2-5 - The Painful Pursuit
While tracking Liza's kidnappers on the ground is easier than following them in the air, it is still no simple task. Though Liza occasionally leaves signs while her captors stop to eat, drink, or rest, the monsters' ability to fly gives them a clear advantage in rugged terrain. To illustrate this, have your players role-play through the following scenarios over the next few days:
Deep Canyon
Today's journey gradually ascends into increasingly rugged terrain with sparse vegetation. Traveling becomes slightly easier until, by evening, you believe you see a small group of humanoid figures flying for a few minutes several miles ahead. An hour later, you discover why. A massive canyon blocks your path, nearly 20 feet wide with steep, treacherous sides and a river rushing far below. Your prey seems to have flown across this obstacle.
The heroes must either climb down and up the near and far sides of the canyon, which takes a perilous hour, or travel eastward for several hours to find a shallower, narrower crossing.
Dangerous Route: Each member must roll a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw to climb safely. Failure results in 2d6 damage from scrapes and bruises.
Safer Route: If the party opts to find an easier crossing, roll 1d4 to determine how many hours they lose by detouring.
Thorny Thicket
As you begin your day's travel, the forest transforms into a sprawling thicket of thorny bushes extending infinitely to the east, south, and west. These tangled, spiked branches reach 10 feet high, with wicked thorns up to 4–6 inches long, oozing green sap. Slightly to the east, you spot another rose-colored ribbon fluttering on the thorns, clearly indicating your target flew straight south over the thicket.
Detour: Traveling east or west to bypass the thicket costs 2d6 hours.
Cut Through: Cutting through the thicket takes 2d4 hours and leaves the players fatigued.
Steep Hillside
The ground rises hundreds of yards, and shrubs grow sparser as you ascend. Finally, you reach a steep hillside covered in loose rocks. The only way forward is through a boulder-strewn area, with rocks the size of houses.
Descending Safely: Players must roll a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw and a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. Failure results in 1d12 damage from slips and falling rocks.
Rushing River
As trees grow taller and further apart, the underbrush gives way to pale grasses and mushrooms sprouting from damp leaf litter. By evening, you come upon a wide, swift-flowing river blocking your path. Its surface is smooth and deep, with occasional leaves or branches swept along, hinting at its strength.
Crossing the River: Each player must pass three DC 16 Athletics checks to swim safely.

Day 6 - The Evil Sacrifice
Six days into your pursuit of the "dragon people," you have faced numerous natural obstacles and come close to catching them several times. Today, though the sky is overcast and threatening rain, the terrain ahead is a broad plain with few obstacles. Luck may finally be on your side, and today could be the day you catch the monsters and rescue the captive.
Build the players' hopes of concluding the long chase today. Tell them they see the monsters in the distance, gradually closing the gap as the day progresses. Have them prepare for battle.
Unknown to the players, one of the dragon-goblin champions, the green-scaled Karlogar, waits high above, ready to ambush them. He does not expect to survive but aims to severely weaken the party through his attacks.
Ambush: At the most dramatic moment, Karlogar dives down from the rain clouds into the party. He begins with a breath weapon attack, then follows with claw strikes, focusing on the strongest and toughest hero to demoralize the group. If successful, he retreats to the clouds, preparing for another strike.
Let players believe the attack is over, then strike again while they are healing. Karlogar disrupts any healing spells and continues hit-and-run tactics until he is defeated.

Day 7 - Further Confusion
The seventh day begins with overcast skies and rain. After crossing yesterday's plains, you enter a lowland swamp teeming with mosquitoes. Mist rises from the ground and the air, mingling with light rain that reduces visibility to mere dozens of yards. You press southward, hoping to close the gap with the monsters.
If desired, the DM can introduce additional natural obstacles or random monster encounters during this day.
By evening, the terrain begins to rise again, and the heroes leave the swamp behind.

Day 8 - Twin Trouble
A week into your vow to rescue young Liza and return her to the village, you find yourself deep in the south, moving along the Ridge of Sorrow - a region separating goblin lands in the west from troll lands in the east. Today, Liza's clues are more frequent, suggesting her captors are monitoring her less. Their path veers west, heading toward the heart of goblin territory.
As the day progresses, Liza's signs grow fresher by the hour. It seems today is the day you finally catch up to the kidnappers!
Unbeknownst to the players, Liza's final two captor-champions have joined two dozen dragon-goblins and handed the girl over to them. These two champions now lie in wait, ready to sacrifice themselves to buy time for their allies to escape.
Encounter: By midday, the trees part to reveal a secluded lakeshore deep in the jungle. Suddenly, a black-scaled, long-necked creature with demonic fangs and claws bursts from the water and attacks!
The Fight: Players face Dark Izlik in one or two combat rounds before Crimson Droggagar descends from above. These two dragon-goblin champions fight fiercely to the death, using every ability they have.

Day 9 - Nearing Rothyra
The monsters delayed you, but they couldn't stop you. Following clear goblin tracks through the jungle, you occasionally find Liza's signs - thank the gods, she's still alive! By mid-afternoon, you hear the sounds of the beasts you're pursuing just ahead, preparing to cross a small stream.
Final Battle: This is the climactic fight players have been waiting for. Let them revel in the hard-earned confrontation. The dragon-goblins lack a coordinated plan, and while some may attempt to flee, they don't prioritize taking Liza with them. She waits in a tree hollow under a large tree, curled up and hoping for rescue.
